# Økonomien blir papir — fire rå dokumenter erstatter Franks notat

**Status:** Approved (Terje, 2026-08-11)
**Author:** Terje + Claude
**Sprint:** SPRINT-001 (Olsen-drift into markup 0.2)
**Visual reference:** `prototypes/paper-reference/in-game-dokumenter.dc.html` (Terje's design-canvas export, 4 sheets — inspiration only, per the lab's wireframe rule)

---

## What this is

`doc_konto` and `doc_papirer` are both `Kind: ØKONOMISK OVERSIKT` — Frank's prose telling
the player the household economy in one paragraph. They are replaced by four raw papers
that print the same information as primary sources, and the player derives the reading.

**Terje's ruling (2026-08-11): no stamps, no Frank marginalia.** The papers stand bare and
printed. Nothing a reader added by hand appears on them.

## Why

**1. The verb needs work to do.** SDD-067 locks the desk's diagnostic verb as
lift-and-couple across registers: hover a phrase, matching phrases in *other* documents
glow, click draws a string. `doc_konto` pre-draws every string. It states the trygd, the
verge arrangement, the husleie, "ikke ett kjøp var hans", and the gap — one paragraph, one
register, nothing left to couple.

**2. The case already asked for these papers.** `# Dispatch: d_konto`:

> Description: Frank ringer til Grete og spør om hun kan skaffe en **bankutskrift**.
> Utskriften kommer i morgen.
> `~ deliver pending_konto_overfort in 1d on ck_overfort`

`pending_konto_overfort` is declared by nothing. It compiles into the slice as a delivery
target with no document behind it (`src/content/blueprint/generated/tinyOlsen.ts:2724`).
The kontoutskrift slot has been empty since the dispatch was written, with Frank's notat
standing in front of it.

**3. The best coupling in the case only exists across sheets.** Elling's årsutskrift shows
`UFØRETRYGD RTV 22,00` in and `FAST OVERF. G. OLSEN (VERGE) 22,00` out on the same date,
four months running, `SALDO 0,00`. Grete's shows `KONTANTUTTAK SKRANKE 30,00` on the 5th.
The kassalapp ties to her card line. No single sheet says *he has no money of his own*.
Three sheets say it together.

**4. `f_gap` duplicated its own hypothesis.** `h_ok_gap`'s title is *"Trygden dekker ikke
boligen. 22 mangler hver måned."* Today the player is handed that as a fact and then
invited to form it as a hypothesis. The swap ends the duplication.

## Documents

Three new kinds. `ØKONOMISK OVERSIKT` loses both its documents, so it leaves
`templates/docs/registry.mjs` along with `templates/docs/kinds/okonomisk-oversikt.mjs`.

| Doc id | Kind | Size | Arrival |
|---|---|---|---|
| `doc_konto_grete` | `KONTOUTSKRIFT` | 800×1131 | `d_konto`, +1d |
| `doc_konto_elling` | `KONTOUTSKRIFT` | 800×1131 | `d_konto`, +1d |
| `doc_strom` | `REGNING` | 800×1131 | new beat day 7, post |
| `doc_kassalapp` | `KASSALAPP` | 300×620 | new beat day 7, shoebox |

Two statements, not one document. Different account holders, different sheets, and the
coupling needs two objects on the desk.

`d_konto` gets two `~ deliver` lines — the effects list takes them (`effects.ts:74` pushes
per line). `pending_konto_overfort` retires and real ids fill the slot. `doc_konto` and
`doc_papirer` have no `arrives:` today, so this is new routing, not a change to existing
routing.

**Known seam:** the kassalapp belongs in the shoebox with Frank at the table, and the case
has no post-death visit dispatch. A day-7 beat delivers it for now. If a post-death
hjemmebesøk ever lands, the kassalapp moves onto it.

## Facts

| Fact | Paper | Printed anchor |
|---|---|---|
| `f_trygd` | 1b | `UFØRETRYGD RTV` rows |
| `f_alt_via_grete` | 1b | footer paragraph `KONTOEN DISPONERES AV VERGE. KORT ER IKKE UTSTEDT.` |
| `f_ingen_matkjop` | 1d | the receipt's `BANKKORT 2,35` line |
| `f_husleie` | 1a | `KONTANTUTTAK SKRANKE` cell |
| `f_gap` | none | derived from `f_trygd` and `f_husleie` |

`KONTOEN DISPONERES AV VERGE. KORT ER IKKE UTSTEDT.` is the load-bearing line in the whole
set. It is printed boilerplate, it looks like nothing, and it proves he cannot buy
anything. It does the work Frank's margin note used to do.

**The footer boilerplate and the address block stay authored paragraphs, not template
chrome.** Only the bank name and the strapline are chrome. Existing paragraph blocks handle
this — no extra grammar needed for it.

### `f_gap` keeps its id and loses its document

`f_gap` has 13 references: three hypotheses (`needs:`), two tiltak, a dispatch gate, a
`visible when:`, two `when:` conditions, and six `Relevant:` lines in the conversation
weave. Deleting it means rewriting all of them. So it stays a fact and stops being a
*document* fact: no `Source:`, and a derived source naming `f_trygd` and `f_husleie`
instead. Every reference survives, and no paper prints a number it does not have. The
compiler emits `source_document_id: ''` plus the derived list.

### `f_husleie` has no name until day 6 (Terje's ruling)

Bare, 1a shows a 30,00 cash withdrawal on the 5th and never prints the word husleie. The
player can only name it as rent by coupling with Bakkerud's letter, and that lands day 6 —
after Grete dies on day 5.

**That is the intent.** The consequence: the economy branch opens on day 6, leaving four
days against the day-10 deadline. This is the case's own thesis. The economy stays
invisible while Grete is alive, because she *is* the mechanism. Statements delivered
pre-death give `f_trygd` and `f_alt_via_grete` only.

### `gate: f_gap` on `d_konto` becomes `gate: f_grete_baerer`

Today the dispatch is gated on the gap — you need the gap to ask for the statement that
proves the gap. `f_grete_baerer` (the doctor's line that she handles his errands and
finances) is the reason to ask.

## Grammar — pipe rows (option A, Terje's pick)

A `|` at line start inside a document body opens a table. Consecutive `|` lines are one
table. A blank line closes it.

```
| DATO  | TEKST                                   | UT [icon=coin] | INN [icon=coin] | SALDO [icon=coin] |
|-------|-----------------------------------------|---------------:|----------------:|------------------:|
| 04.01 | PENSJON RTV                             |                |           31,00 |             37,40 |
| 05.01 | [KONTANTUTTAK SKRANKE](fact:f_husleie)  |          30,00 |                 |             29,40 |
| 07.01 | MATSENTRALEN AMMERUD                  |           2,35 |                 |             27,05 |
```

- A separator row of `-` and `|` marks the row above as the header. `---:` right-aligns.
- Cells split on `|`, outer pipes optional, cells trimmed. `\|` escapes a literal pipe.
- A cell takes the same run grammar as prose — `[text](fact:id)` anchors and `[icon=coin]`
  work unchanged.
- A row whose cell count differs from the header warns as `DOC_TABLE_RAGGED`. Never fatal
  (project rule: stubs and drift warn, never block the compile).
- **The coin goes in the column header, once, not on every row.** The uncommitted
  `shared.mjs` change swapped the `¤` glyph for the real game sprite, so `UT [icon=coin]`
  gives the canvas look with the desk's own coin.

### Emitted shapes

`labContent` blocks gain a `type`. Existing paragraph blocks become `type: 'para'`. Tables
emit:

```js
{ id: 'doc_konto_grete_t1', type: 'table',
  align: ['left','left','right','right','right'],
  header: [[run], ...], rows: [[[run], ...], ...] }
```

Every cell is a run list, so UV measurement is unchanged — `measureFacts` queries
`[data-fact-id]` anywhere in the DOM, and a span inside a `<td>` yields the same
`getClientRects()` line boxes as a span inside a `<p>`.

The slice's `body_bbcode` grows a `[table=N][cell]…[/cell][/table]` form. It is flat
whitespace-collapsed prose today, so pipe rows would collapse into garbage without this.

Both consumers of the block shape live in this repo: `templates/docs/render-doc.mjs` and
the script-editor preview.

## Templates

Three new kind modules plus `KIND_SIZES` entries. Ported from the canvas export minus the
canvas-only presentation: no `transform: rotate()`, no `box-shadow`, no `sc-if` wear/stamp
layers. The bake pages are axis-aligned because the desk mesh maps the texture
edge-to-edge and the desk supplies its own light.

`KASSALAPP` opts out of the standard `.page` ink border — the sawtooth `clip-path` edge
would cut through it.

**Fonts:** `Archivo` is not embedded, and 1c's letterhead is grotesk, so vendor
`Archivo-400.ttf` and `Archivo-700.ttf` beside the existing six in `templates/docs/fonts/`.
`Courier Prime` maps onto the already-embedded `Special Elite`.

## Content fixes riding along

1. **Digit bug.** Fact summaries and one hypothesis lost a leading digit — `2` and `3`
   where document prose says `22` and `30`. Sites: `tiny-olsen.case.md` lines 66, 80, 86,
   87, 113, 450, 451. The raw papers make this visible immediately, because the arithmetic
   is now printed.
2. **1d contradicts 1a.** The receipt says `KONTANT 2,50 / VEKSEL 0,10` for `07.01.99
   10:42`, total `2,40`. Grete's statement shows `07.01 MATSENTRALEN AMMERUD 2,35`. A
   cash purchase leaves no statement line, and the totals disagree. Change the receipt to
   `BANKKORT 2,35`, matching her row exactly. That turns a contradiction into a three-way
   lock: her receipt, her card line, his `KORT ER IKKE UTSTEDT`.
3. **`UBETALT` stamp dropped from 1c costs nothing.** The print already carries
   `2. GANGS VARSEL` and threatens frakobling under § 7.
