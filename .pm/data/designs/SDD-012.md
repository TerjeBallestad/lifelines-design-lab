# Ti dager: one door opening. A clean-spine HTML probe for the prologue loop

Brainstormed with Terje 2026-09-03. Track F. Supersedes the "step 3" probe idea in `docs/golden-path-replan-2026-09-01.md`. Answers the handoff's gating question.

## What broke when blueprint_v1 met the sim (Terje, 2026-09-03)

Not one mechanic. Every mechanic that asks the player to spend or gate got ignored: action budget, coins, hypothesis gate, clocks, the tiltak catalogue. The blueprint itself showed this before the sim did. The sim seam felt disconnected because the sim did not know a decision had been made, so the player watched a needs-driven apartment that answered nothing.

What works, proven in the blueprint and in Godot: the documents, the story, talking to the characters and slowly getting the full picture, and lifting fact cards from documents.

## Rulings from the session

- The payoff of Lifelines is growth: watching a broken man take steps toward an independent, healthy life. The ten-day slice is the prologue. It shows one rung of the ladder so the player believes the ladder exists.
- The rung is contact. Elling starts behind a locked door and an unanswered phone. By day 9 he may let Frank in.
- The skill model is a developmental ladder (Piaget): complex skills build on basic ones. Elling is stuck at the stage where the skills are "clean your room, eat your vegetables, do what mother says". SDD-039's twelve skills exist in core-loop code with an EMPTY prerequisite map. The ladder is the unmodelled piece. It is a core-loop design question, NOT part of this probe.
- The slice does not fix the ending. Four verdicts stay. A normal playthrough tends toward institutionalisation, by consent or by force. A skilled player can scaffold Elling into staying. A negligent player can leave him there against the evidence.
- The Olsen pack prose and data shapes are not reused. Fresh spine, fresh manuscript, per the handoff decision.
- The only feel gate: do these mechanics make you want to press Avslutt dagen and see what lands? Diagnosis goes in play notes after the fact, not in the design.

## Cut from the slice

Action budget, coins, hypothesis gate, clocks, tiltak catalogue, vedtak form as a spending screen, tillit as a resource, TANKER page, skill panel, needs display, timeline, room view. A tiltak survives only as the brief the player gives Frank.

## Approach chosen: B, new page on a clean spine, content as a separate file

Rejected: A (fork vedtak_form_probe: inherits Olsen prose and shapes), C (paper greybox: Terje's day table already is it).

## Section 1: content file `case.json`

Seven shapes, nothing the slice does not use. Prose in markdown. Fact anchors are ordinary links: `[uåpnet post på skoskapet](f_post)`.

- **Case.** Id, title, ten days, opening state, list of verdicts.
- **Document.** Id, kind, sender, prose with anchors. Arrival is not stored here; triggers and visits deliver documents.
- **Fact.** Id, card face. Optional Frank line (what he says when handed the card) and what it unlocks: a call, a brief, or nothing. Replaces mint, recipes, hypotheses.
- **Call.** Who, availability condition, short branching dialogue. Nodes have lines with anchors and one to three replies. Facts lift live. The Elling call has no tree; its outcome is read off the door ladder.
- **Visit.** A brief (Frank's approach in one line), requirements, a beat script (speaker + text + anchors). Branches on the door ladder, may move it one step. Always ends by delivering a Visit-notat document. Kartlegg økonomi is a visit with no scene: spends the day, paper lands tomorrow.
- **Trigger.** Id, condition, floor, ceiling, effect. Condition grammar has five words: `has f_x`, `fired id`, `visited v_x`, `door >= n`, joined with `and`. Effects deliver a document or add a Dagsrapport line.
- **Verdict.** Four pages, variants keyed on door ladder step and on whether the player witnessed the change or only read about it.

**Hidden state: the door ladder.** Five steps named by behaviour: silent, at the door, speaks through the door, door on a crack, lets Frank in. Phone follows the same ladder (same rung). Visits and Elling calls read it; only visits move it. It is never shown as a number.

## Section 2: engine and day loop

Pure functions over one state object, no DOM (runs under vitest).

State: day, field action spent, documents (read flags), facts held, calls made, visits done, triggers fired (with day), door step, pending deliveries, Frank transcript, Dagsrapport lines, witnessed flags, verdict.

Free desk verbs, unlimited: open document, lift fact, hand fact to Frank, ring an available number, read Dagsrapport.

One field verb a day: send Frank with a brief. Plays the visit beats now, may move the ladder, delivers the Visit-notat at once, spends the day.

Avslutt dagen: (1) evaluate unfired triggers: fire if condition holds and day >= floor, or day >= ceiling; queue effects. (2) day + 1, field action back. (3) morning: queued documents land; Dagsrapport only if it has lines. Day 10: verdict screen instead of the morning.

Elling call: available once Grete is admitted; returns one authored line per ladder step; never moves the ladder. This is how growth is legible without a number.

Not in the engine: needs, clocks, coins, action counter, sim tick. Consequence is authored in visit scripts and trigger effects. If the loop is fun, that authored consequence is the spec the real sim must meet (DD-109, DD-112, DD-130).

## Section 3: screens (one page, desk is home)

- **Desk.** Top bar: Dag N, Frank out today or not, Avslutt dagen. Inbox left (paper, unread on top). Hand right (fact cards). Phone bottom (available numbers). Frank on the desk as the card drop target.
- **Reader.** One document as a page. Anchors underlined; click lifts the card and highlights the passage yellow (visual reference: `../lifelines-core-loop/wireframes/20260506_case_session_spike_v3.html`).
- **Frank.** Transcript pane. Dropping a card appends his line. An unlocked brief appears as a Send Frank button. No visit without a brief.
- **Call and visit.** Same overlay (blueprint_v2 scene device): one beat at a time, Videre, Hopp over. Calls show replies as buttons; visits show none. Anchors in beats are liftable.
- **Verdict.** Day 10. Four options as a form, hand shown beside it. Signing shows the consequence page and ends the game.

Styling: paper and ink language from blueprint_v1. No new visual work.

## Verification

Feel gate (the only one): does Terje want to press Avslutt dagen and see what lands? Terje plays; that is the review.

Automated (vitest, engine is pure): content validator (every anchor names an existing fact, every trigger effect names an existing document, every visit branch reaches the ladder steps it claims); idle run (no field actions) reaches the idle ending on day 10; golden path reaches ladder step five by day 9; no trigger fires twice.

Edge cases the content must cover: player never lifts the fact that unlocks the first brief (Frank offers a fallback brief by day 3); Frank sent to the flat after Grete is admitted; Elling rung on day 1; every scene skipped (the Visit-notat alone must carry the door change).
