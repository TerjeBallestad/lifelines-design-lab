      # Hardest to express (design work for the grill)

      1. **`frank_chat`** — nested, paced answer arrays with branching follow-ups. Three generations layered: legacy flat `answer`, `answer_lines[]`, `followups[]` each with `lines[]` + optional `tanke` sting. `c_dor_glott` has `answer: ""` (answer_lines is primary). Plus `pays_fact: "f_dor_glott"` on `c_avstand` — a chat entry that MINTS a fact, inverting the `needs:` direction. (`75816564`, `635c486a`)
      2. **`calls`** — card-keyed exchanges: each entry keyed by the played `card_id`, replies are speaker-tagged line arrays (`who: "du"`) that may lift facts mid-call (`fact_id` on a line). Counterpart: `reveals_event: "call:grete"` on the fact side. (`141adbc1`, `1f5a5008`, `6d718314`)
      3. **`recipes`** — fact-pair → question crafting with THREE parallel copy surfaces: `questions[].teaser` duplicated verbatim into `recipes[].reading`, `facts[].card_line`, `questions[].card_title/card_sub`, plus two global fallback lines. 0.2 should let one block own all of it and derive `reading`. (`acbc692b`)
      4. **`frank_proposals`** — 24 ranked lines keyed to a bare `handbok_id` slug (not a tiltak id), relevance = fact-id set OR category set, plus a hand-maintained global `order` integer. (`75816564`)
      5. **`open_conversation` effect** — carries planner metadata outside `args`, and `sim_hook_id` is a namespace the .case.md never names. (`9fc89bd6`)

      Two traps: `leads` union type (pick one form, migrate) and sparse-vs-default emission (`weight`, `max_value`, `good_segment_size: 0` vs default 4).
