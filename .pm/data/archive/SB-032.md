The work: the canvas inspector becomes the edit surface. Per-kind forms (fact: label/summary/category/quote; question: prompt/teaser/card; hypothesis: title/summary; tiltak: title/slot/cost/description; dispatch/clock fields). A field commit → markup patch (SB-031) → POST /__save-case → recompile → re-render, selection preserved. Diagnostics for the edited block show inline in the inspector.

DRAFT SAFETY (hard requirement, learned on SB-025): unsaved canvas edits live in localStorage from v1 and survive a vite reload — same draft-key pattern as the script editor.

Why it blocks: 'edit in place' is the core verb of DD-003; the feel probe needs it.

Acceptance: jsdom test edits a fact label through the form and the recompiled node re-renders; draft restores after reload; no-op focus/blur writes nothing.
