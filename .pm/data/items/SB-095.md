## Question

Re-price the Olsen case economy to whole-number fantasy coins. The decimal comma is Norwegian bank formatting — «22,00» reads as kr no matter the icon. Whole numbers kill the tell (SB-093 follow-on, Terje 2026-08-12).

## Ruling (Terje, 2026-08-12)

- Baseline: one cheap grocery item = 1 coin (the kassalapp floor).
- Compressed ratio, NOT realistic 500:1 — saldo columns must stay 2–3 digits so the arithmetic chains stay readable at a glance.
- Approved anchor values: basket 10, strømregning 18, trygd 90, pensjon 125, rent 120.
