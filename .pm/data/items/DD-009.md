**Decision:** The visit-system rewrite adopts the stage/role/duty model (RimWorld-ritual family): a visit is an ordered list of stages; each stage assigns each role (frank/grete/elling) one declarative duty; characters' own AI executes duties on a single priority axis; stages end on authored endTriggers (roles-arrived, duration, event) and fail on authored failTriggers with a grace period. The serial global step queue in social_visit_director.gd is retired, along with its hand-compiled band-aids (grete_hold_door/host_hold/tea_hold, await_answer, no_wait).

**Alternatives considered:** (1) Keep the serial queue, widen the markup vocabulary — rejected: every hold/stall band-aid is an artifact of forcing parallel behavior through a serial pipe; no shipped game runs multi-character scenes this way (SB-105). (2) Sims-style pushed interactions into per-character queues — rejected: better suited to open-ended socializing than authored named-cast scenes, and leaves failure handling in code rather than in the authored data.

**Why this one:** Maps ~1:1 onto Terje's authoring sentence ("Frank: go to Elling, when there say this; both to the sofa" — stages are the 'then' boundaries, RolesArrived is the meeting point). Proven pure-data authoring format, so it compiles from Kildeverket markup like every other family. Failure becomes authored edges (fail triggers, call-off path) — prior art shows skipping this pins NPCs, which is exactly the current disease.

**Consequences:** The hosting spine stops being sacred engine code — arrival/exit become authored stages in the same grammar. The markup Visit step-kinds (line/urgent/queue_elling) and their emitters are superseded. GAP-006's when-guard debt is resolved by the new trigger grammar rather than patched. Engine work lands in lifelines-core-loop and needs its own SDD.

Source: SB-098 grill + SB-105 prior-art research (docs/research-scripted-scenes-prior-art.md).
