SB-077 verdict graduated this: before any diet ruling, pin the facts. For every block kind in the case markup (case, document, fact, question, hypothesis, tiltak, dispatch, clock, the weave blocks): which fields/properties are (a) consumed by the Godot sim, (b) consumed by a lab surface (canvas, script lens, playtest, previews), (c) compiler-required (validation/emit), (d) authored but consumed nowhere. Same tiering as the SB-101 strings liveness map, applied to the schema instead of the string families. Output: one table, per kind per field, with the consumer named — the [grill] content diet ruling reads it.

Why it blocks: the diet grill without this inventory is opinion vs opinion; with it, half the axe-candidates fall out as facts (consumed nowhere = free cut).
