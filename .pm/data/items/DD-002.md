Context: 0.2 weave (§8) describes more than the SB-584/587 runtime can play (gathers, deep nesting, sequences, asked-counts, several guard terms have no runtime op).

Decision: the emit boundary is the runtime's actual capability. Playable constructs compile to JSON. Unplayable constructs parse and validate but emit a compiler warning and NO JSON. ~ pay stays one sigil with surface-decided behavior. * -> is not mapped to soft-reject.

Why: emitting forward-compatible JSON the runtime silently ignores makes the author believe the game plays their content. A warning is honest, and the warning list is the engine-work backlog. Extends the reference law 'stubs, never fatal'.

Alternative rejected: emit-and-ignore (silent no-play is the worst authoring failure mode).
