One attemptable unit inside an Oppdrag: it owns one scene (a stage/duty list in the TERM-003..006 grammar) plus authored requirements (conditions for Frank to attempt it) and a yield (facts or unlocks it can produce). Its time cost is never authored - it derives from the scene (SB-114 ruling). The director picks among goals whose requirements hold and whose cost fits the remaining visit time; an Oppdrag is composed of goals and has no authored spine between arrival and exit. Authoring surface (SB-114): '## Goal: name' + plan: + need: (presence, facts, 'denied <goal>', 'not' negation) + yield:.
