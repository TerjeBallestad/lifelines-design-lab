One declarative behavior a visit stage assigns to one character, executed by the character's own AI (DD-009). The six duty words are goto, stay, say, converse, do, free. Seat/posture are parameters, not duties. Duties are sticky: an unnamed character carries the previous duty until overwritten or released. free releases the character to their own decision engine mid-visit (visit end and call-off release implicitly). Avoid as duty words: host, escort, wait, hold.

Naming ruling (SB-107 probe session): the markup names characters (frank/grete/elling), never abstract parts. These visits are authored named-cast scenes, and the dialogue makes them unrecastable anyway. "Role" is the engine-side term for the slot a character occupies in a running scene (as in the role-lost fail trigger), not a markup concept. A reusable spine, if ever real, arrives later as a template layer.
