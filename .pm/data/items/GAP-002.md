## What

SDD-011 retires `f_gap`'s document. The fact keeps its id, loses `Source:`, and gains a derived list naming `f_trygd` and `f_husleie`. The compiler emits `source_document_id: ''` plus that list.

Nothing pays it after that.

`case_engine.gd:lift_fact` is reached from exactly four places (verified 2026-08-11 in `../lifelines-core-loop/scripts/case_files/case_engine.gd`):

- a document run hotspot
- a call line `fact_id`
- a chat entry `pays_fact`
- an event delta `reveal_fact_id`

A derived fact hits none of them. `# Recipe: f_a + f_b` does not pay either — the recipe emitter carries `~ open q_x` and Frank lines only (`src/compiler/emit.ts`, recipes section).

## The debt

The design-lab side of SDD-011 emits `derived_from` and teaches `payableFacts` (`src/compiler/lints.ts:72`) to count a derived fact as payable when its inputs are. That keeps the advisory lints honest and is all this repo can do.

**core-loop owes the engine rule:** when every fact in a derived fact's `derived_from` list is lifted, lift the derived fact. Until that lands, the compiled slice describes a branch the game cannot open.
