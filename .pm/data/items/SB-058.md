## Question
How much sim does the Playtest lens run? The slice carries predicates (reveal_when, gates), effects, clocks, dispatches, and day beats. Options range from reveal-gating only (facts unlock questions, nothing else advances) to a full day loop with clocks and effects. Where exactly is the "not a second game implementation" line (lab pinned rule)?

## Why it blocks
The loop-skeleton choice and the playthrough probe both depend on how much state the lens may hold. Deciding this first stops the lens from drifting into a Godot replacement one feature at a time.
