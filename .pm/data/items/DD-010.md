**Decision:** An Oppdrag is composed of goals (TERM-007). A goal owns one scene in the TERM-003..006 stage/duty/trigger grammar plus authored requirements, cost, and yield. The visit has no authored spine between arrival and exit: the director walks the player's ordered goal list, skips goals whose requirements fail or whose cost does not fit the remaining time, and plays the first that passes. A fail trigger inside a goal's scene denies that goal for the current visit and returns control to the selection loop - the call-off (TERM-006, amended) fires only at visit level: nothing attemptable, time spent, or a visit-level failure. Denial is per-visit by default; requirements may reference denial facts to keep a goal closed. The player orders goals only at planning time (phone/case screen, diegetic as Frank's plan, 3-5 items) - no live reordering during the visit. Selection uses the strict player order, no utility scoring. Results need no new system: yield is the score, and the visit report shows planned vs achieved vs denied.

**Alternatives considered:** (1) Goal as a rename of Oppdrag with fallback Oppdrag - rejected: fallbacks like 'explore the apartment' would need full mission dressing. (2) Weighted utility selection (RimWorld think-tree style) - rejected: the player must predict Frank from their own ordering, or denials read as AI mood instead of consequence. (3) Live mid-visit reordering - rejected: turns every denial into pause-and-decide and kills the watch-Frank-adapt payoff. (4) Keeping every fail routed to the call-off (SB-106 letter) - rejected: it forbids exactly the improvisation the goal tier exists for.

**Why this one:** It lands the two-tier authorship lesson from SB-105 (premise and pressure above, choreography below) in the existing vocabulary without reopening SB-106: fail triggers ARE denial detection, and the call-off keeps one job one level up. Dynamic in-apartment variety comes from requirements and costs, not from new machinery.

**Consequences:** The three # Visit: oppdrag_* blocks in tiny-olsen dissolve into goals with requirements/cost/yield - new markup grammar needed (goal-tier sibling of the SB-107 stage syntax). TERM-006 amended (goal denial vs visit call-off). The planning screen is game UI, engine-side SDD territory. The visit report gains planned/achieved/denied for free.
