Graduated from the SB-088 seam list (item 3). The drawer FIELDS form edits structured fields only. Editing a document's prose still means switching to SCRIPT mode. Extending the form to prose (a textarea over the body? per-paragraph rows?) is new design, not polish — and it may not be wanted: SCRIPT is the resting mode by Terje's ruling, and prose is what SCRIPT is good at. The question: is a prose-capable FIELDS form worth its surface, or is the current split (fields → form, prose → script) the design? Blocks nothing.
