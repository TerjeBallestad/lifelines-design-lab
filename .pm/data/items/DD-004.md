Ruling (SB-090, 2026-08-11): the UI-chrome/scene text layer (~20 scripts/ui/*.gd, ~120+ baked .tscn text= entries, English-remnant surfaces, .tres display_names) does NOT join SDD-130's '# Strings:' family. It gets its own later SDD (localization plumbing, needs a .tscn writer the compiler does not have).

Narrow exception: divergent duplicates that shadow a migrated named family (raw frank-action navn literals, håndbok category words, SI FRA vs HEVDE SEG class) die in SB-072's wave — migrating a family while a raw copy survives elsewhere plants the next drift.

Trade-off: folding the chrome layer in would roughly double SB-072 and stall the map behind one giant step. Staying hardcoded forever was rejected — the SI FRA/HEVDE SEG split shows the drift cost is already real.
