Parse the SB-107 stage grammar in Visit blocks and emit stage/duty/trigger data for the pack. The surface shape is ruled (SB-107 resolution comment): anonymous == stages with compile-time ids, '- character: duty' with goto/stay/say/converse/do/free, quoteless dialogue with trailing key=value params, bare character names as goto targets, self-terminating say/converse, -> arrived|duration|event, !> timeout|unreachable|role-lost (grace=), ## Call-off section.

Compile-time validation (all pack errors): a stage with nothing self-terminating must author an end trigger, one duty per character per stage, a room may never share a name with a character, unknown duty/trigger words fail, unknown activities emit as stubs (warning, not error). Typography lint applies to dialogue lines as usual.

Decide the emit shape with an eye on the core-loop stage director SDD - the engine consumes exactly this data. The probe's parser (prototypes/visit-syntax/index.html) is the reference implementation of the line grammar.
