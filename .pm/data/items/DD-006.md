Terje, SB-094 grill 2026-08-12. The rule: if the paper prints the meaning (UFØRETRYGD RTV), the hotspot pays the real fact directly — as today. If the row is mute (KONTANTUTTAK SKRANKE 30,00 — a number with no stated meaning), the hotspot pays a raw card, and playing that card into a conversation pays the real fact via a card-keyed branch (TERM-001) with ~ pay. Every fact, direct or named, remains playable in conversation afterwards. Why not uniform-through-conversation: an NPC restating what the paper already prints makes the play verb busywork, and it doubles the weave authoring. Why not all-direct: mute rows carry the case's real deductions (f_husleie), and naming them is the conversation verb's job. Consequence for tiny-olsen: f_trygd stays a direct lift on doc_konto_elling; the skranke row lifts a new raw-card fact, and Grete's and Frank's weaves gain branches that pay f_husleie.
