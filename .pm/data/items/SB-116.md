The SB-077 verdict, in Terje's words: the underlying problem is the content itself — too much. The goal is content served bite-sized, one problem at a time, and a coherent unified game experience is still elusive. Every board-scoping tool missed because it managed volume instead of removing it.

The decision only Terje can make: given the liveness inventory, what goes? Candidate axes: whole kinds (do recipes/proposals/dispatches earn their place?), per-kind fields (which authored properties never change a play experience?), and case-size norms (what is the bite-sized unit — per-question? per-day? — that an author writes and a player digests as one problem).
